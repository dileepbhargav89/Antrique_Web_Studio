# 04 — UX Documentation

## Emotion-annotated journeys
Every design decision traces to a user anxiety. Primary journey low point is the
**commit moment** (high intent, low patience) → wizard is one-question-per-screen.
Post-sale low point is the **delivery-wait** ("is anything happening?") → portal
is status-first.

## Wireframes
Structural layout schematics (region hierarchy, CTA placement, intent) for the
conversion spine (Home, Service Detail, Quote wizard, Portal Dashboard) and
supporting pages (Industry Detail, Work, Pricing, Contact), plus portal screens
(project tracking, invoice+payment, ticket detail). Neutral-token structural
layouts; brand tokens applied at hi-fi.

## Micro-interactions
Purposeful only — button press, CTA hover, field focus, inline validation,
metric count-up, accordion, wizard progress, skeleton→content. Reduced-motion-safe.

## Loading UX
Skeletons over spinners; prioritized hero (LCP); fixed image aspect ratios;
progressive portal widgets. Never a blank screen or unbounded spinner.

## Conversion optimization
One primary action per viewport; persistent low-friction quote path; trust at the
point of hesitation; no dead ends; no dark patterns; no auto-submit. Measured via
quote-start rate and per-step drop-off.

## Responsive
Mobile-first baseline (audience skews mobile). Tablet explicitly designed (not
desktop-shrunk). Identical capability across devices.
